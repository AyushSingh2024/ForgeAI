# Data Systems 210: Course Handbook (fictional course, autumn term)

## Assessment
- Lab assignments: 30 percent (best 5 of 6 labs count)
- Group project: 30 percent
- Final exam: 40 percent
A minimum of 40 percent in the final exam is required to pass the course.

## Deadlines
- Lab 1: 2026-09-21, Lab 2: 2026-10-05, Lab 3: 2026-10-19, Lab 4: 2026-11-02, Lab 5: 2026-11-16, Lab 6: 2026-11-30
- Project proposal: 2026-10-12; final project report: 2026-12-11
- Final exam: 2026-12-18 at 09:00 in Hall B

## Late submissions
Late labs lose 10 percent of the mark per day, up to 3 days; after 3 days they receive zero. Extensions are only
granted for documented illness or emergencies and must be requested before the deadline.
