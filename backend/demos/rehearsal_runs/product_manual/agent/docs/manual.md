# Aurelia AX-200 Owner's Manual (fictional product)

## 1. Setup
Fill the water tank with fresh cold water up to the MAX line (1.8 litres). Place the machine on a flat surface at
least 10 cm from the wall. Before first use, run two empty cycles by pressing the hot water button for 30 seconds.

## 2. Daily cleaning
Empty the drip tray and the grounds container every day. Rinse the portafilter after each use. Wipe the steam wand
immediately after frothing milk and purge it for 2 seconds.

## 3. Descaling
Descale every 2 months with soft water, or every month with hard water (above 14 °dH). Use 500 ml of water with one
sachet of AX descaler. The descaling programme takes about 25 minutes. The DESCALE light turns off when it is done.

## 4. Error codes
- E01: water tank empty or not seated. Refill the tank and push it down until it clicks.
- E02: brew unit blocked. Remove the brew unit, rinse it under warm water and let it dry for 10 minutes.
- E05: temperature sensor fault. Switch off and contact an authorised service centre.
- E07: grinder overload. Remove foreign objects from the bean hopper; do not use oily or caramelised beans.

## 5. Warranty
The machine has a 2-year warranty. The warranty does not cover damage caused by not descaling, by using
non-original parts, or by repairs done outside an authorised service centre.
